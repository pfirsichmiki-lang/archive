Profile  /  略歴

三木晋吉（みき しんきち／Mike Shinkichi Miki）。ニューヨークを拠点に、ザ・ボールドウィン・グループの常務取締役、Global Japanese Practice 共同統括として、米国に進出する日本企業の企業保険とリスクマネジメントを担当しています。

1987年に早稲田大学を卒業して東京海上へ入社、以後30年以上を国際部門で過ごしました。フィリピン5年、シンシナティ5年（支店長）、ロサンゼルス6年（Head of Western Region）を経て、タイ国東京海上火災保険の社長、のちアジア統括。タイではセーフティー・インシュランスとの統合を、ミャンマーではシュエタング・グループとの合弁会社設立を主導しました。学生時代からのバスケットボール選手で、1980年代半ばの早稲田大学を経て、1980年代後半から1990年代初頭にかけて日本リーグ（現Bリーグ）東京海上ビッグブルーに背番号13で在籍。1965年ロサンゼルス生まれ、ニュージャージー在住。英文では Shinkichi Miki、Mike Miki とも表記されます。

Mike Shinkichi Miki (三木晋吉) is Managing Director and Co-Leader of the Global Japanese Practice at The Baldwin Group in New York, where he advises Japanese corporations operating in the United States on commercial insurance and risk management.

Before joining Baldwin in 2022, he spent over three decades with Tokio Marine in international roles, serving as President of Tokio Marine Insurance (Thailand) and later as Head of Asia. In Thailand he led the merger with Safety Insurance that created Tokio Marine Safety Insurance, one of the country’s largest general insurers, and in Myanmar he led the establishment of Grand Guardian Tokio Marine, the general insurance joint venture with the Shwe Taung Group. His overseas postings began directly after his playing career ended: five years in the Philippines, five years in Cincinnati as Branch Manager, and six years in Los Angeles as Head of Western Region. Born in Los Angeles in 1965, he graduated from Waseda University Senior High School in 1983 and from Waseda University in 1987.

Earlier in his career, from the late 1980s into the early 1990s, he played basketball in Japan's national league — the forerunner of today's B.LEAGUE — for Tokio Marine Big Blue, after playing for Waseda University in the mid-1980s. He works in both Japanese and English, and is based in New Jersey.

Published work in English from his years in Asia and the United States appears under the name Shinkichi Miki. The materials below are largely in Japanese; each entry carries an English summary.
