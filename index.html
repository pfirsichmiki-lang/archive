Profile  /  英文略歴

Mike Shinkichi Miki (三木晋吉) is Managing Director and Co-Leader of the Global Japanese Practice at The Baldwin Group in New York, where he advises Japanese corporations operating in the United States on commercial insurance and risk management.

Before joining Baldwin in 2022, he spent over three decades with Tokio Marine in international roles, serving as President of Tokio Marine Insurance (Thailand) and later as Head of Asia. In Thailand he led the merger with Safety Insurance that created Tokio Marine Safety Insurance, one of the country’s largest general insurers, and in Myanmar he led the establishment of Grand Guardian Tokio Marine, the general insurance joint venture with the Shwe Taung Group. Earlier postings took him to Los Angeles, Cincinnati and Manila. Born in Los Angeles in 1965, he graduated from Waseda University Senior High School in 1983 and from Waseda University in 1987.

Earlier in his career he played basketball in Japan's national league — the forerunner of today's B.LEAGUE — for Tokio Marine Big Blue. He works in both Japanese and English, and is based in New Jersey.

Published work in English from his years in Asia and the United States appears under the name Shinkichi Miki. The materials below are largely in Japanese; each entry carries an English summary.
